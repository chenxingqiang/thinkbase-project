\noindent \textbf{Introduction}

\noindent All manuscript must be written in English, the same applies to the tables and figures. The size of the paper is expected to be from 6 up to 10 pages.

\underline{The paper must be submitted in Word format}\textbf{.} % Please keep a second copy of your manuscript in Word or Latex (pdflatex). When receiving the paper, the organizers assume that the corresponding authors grant us the paper copyright for the conference book or journal. If the authors use tables or figures from other Publications, they must ask the corresponding publishers the publishing rights to include it in the paper.


Do not use \textbf{boldface} typing or CAPITAL LETTERS for emphasizing a word or phrase except for section headings (cf. remarks on section headings, below).


\noindent \textbf{Article Organization}

\noindent The section headings are in boldface capital and lowercase letters. No numbers on the headings. You can keep simple without latex sections.

 \textbf{Second level Heading:} Second level headings are typed as part of the succeeding paragraph (like the subsection heading of this paragraph). Please see some pieces of advice about the article organization: 

\begin{itemize}
\item Page Numbers: Please, do not number your paper.
\item Tables: Tables (refer with: Table 1, Table 2, ...) should be presented  as part of the text, but in such a way as to avoid confusion with the text. A descriptive title should be placed above each table. Units in tables should be given in square brackets {[}meV{]}.
\item Special Sign should always be written in with the fonts Times New Roman or Arial, especially in the figures and tables.
\item Macros: Do not use any macros for the figures and tables.
\item If you follow the ``checklist'' your paper will conform to the
  requirements of the publisher and facilitate heavy publication
  process. Thank you for your help in this matter.
\end{itemize}

Figures (refer with: Fig. 1, Fig. 2, ...) also should be presented as part of the text, leaving enough space so that the caption will not be confused with the text. The caption should be self-contained and placed directly below the figure, as presented in Fig.\ref{fig:enter-label}. Generally, only original drawings or photographic reproductions are acceptable. Please take care to insert the figures in correct alignment with the text. The pictures should have a minimum resolution of 300 dpi in the exported pdf. Color figures are welcome.
\begin{figure}[htp!]

Equations (refer with: Eq. 1, Eq. 2, ...) should be indented 5 mm. There should be one line of space above the equation and one line of space below it before the text continues, as seen Eq.\ref{eq:1}. The equations have to be numbered sequentially, and the number put in parentheses at the right-hand edge of the text. %dont leave space between equations
\begin{equation} \label{eq:1}
c^2=a^2+b^2
\end{equation}

\noindent \textbf{Literature References}

\noindent All references are cited in the text just by square brackets \cite{Star1} for article and book \cite{book}. The references are to be numbered in the order in which they are cited in the text and listed in References section. All authors of each reference must be cited.
